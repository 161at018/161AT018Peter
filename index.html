Welcome to 161AT018 Peter's Website

Hello! I am Peter, a radio operator from Poland and a proud member of the Alfa Tango International Group.

This website is dedicated to my passion for 11m band radio communications. Here you will find my equipment list, QTH details, and the full prefix directory.

Radio DX-ing is more than a hobby for me—it's a way to connect with people from all over the world. Thanks for visiting!
