About the area

Bystrzyca Kłodzka is a beautiful, historic town located in the Kłodzko Valley, at the confluence of the Nysa Kłodzka and Bystrzyca rivers. The town is surrounded by the Sudetes mountain range, which provides a unique and challenging environment for radio propagation.

The historic architecture and the mountainous landscape make it one of the most picturesque spots in South-Western Poland. I look forward to catching you on the air from this location!
