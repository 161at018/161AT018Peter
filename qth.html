Welcome to the description of my radio station's home. I am operating from a very interesting part of Poland.

City: Bystrzyca Kłodzka

Region: Lower Silesia (Dolnośląskie)

Country: Poland

Grid Locator: JO80IH

Location map of Bystrzyca Kłodzka, Poland

Geographical Context

My QTH is situated in the Kłodzko Valley, surrounded by the beautiful Sudetes Mountains. The town itself is a historic gem, known for its well-preserved medieval layout and unique architecture. The mountainous terrain provides varied and often challenging radio propagation conditions, which makes every DX contact even more exciting!
