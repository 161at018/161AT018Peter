About my location

Bystrzyca Kłodzka is a historic town in South-Western Poland. Located in a mountainous area (Sudetes), it offers great opportunities for radio propagation, though the surrounding peaks can sometimes be a challenge! The town is famous for its medieval architecture and beautiful landscapes.
