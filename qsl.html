QSL Exchange Information

QSL STATUS: 100% RETURN

Receiving and sending QSL cards is a very important part of my radio hobby. I value every contact and I am always happy to exchange traditional paper cards.

How to exchange cards

Direct QSL Please send your card to my postal address. I do not require contributions (standard AT rules), but a self-addressed envelope (SAE) is always appreciated to speed up the process.
Via Manager / Bureau If a manager is active for a specific activation, please follow the specific instructions provided during the contact.

Important Note

Please make sure the QSO data (Time in UTC, Date, Frequency, Mode, and Signal Report) is clearly written on your card. This helps me verify our contact in my logbook quickly.

Address info is available upon request via Email or Facebook.
